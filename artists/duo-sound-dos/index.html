The artists

Two artists, one musical conversation.

Rubén Aguiar is a Cuban poet, songwriter, composer and guitarist, born in Matanzas. A graduate of the Matanzas School of Arts, he began performing Cuban traditional music as a child and has developed a long career as a musician, composer and performer in Cuba, Spain and Miami.

Bárbara Milián is a Cuban composer, singer and guitarist. She co-founded Dúo Sound Dos with Rubén in Miami. Together, they present a shared musical project rooted in song and guitar.

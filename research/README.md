## Editorial standard

- Separate published evidence from proposals and hypotheses.
- Link primary research wherever possible.
- Do not present conceptual frameworks as validated discoveries.
- Preserve uncertainty, limitations, and counterexamples.
- Prefer experiments and inspectable results over unsupported claims.

These are working papers, not final claims. Corrections and substantive criticism are welcome.
